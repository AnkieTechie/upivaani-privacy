Privacy Policy for UPI Vaani

Effective Date: 2025-08-20 (Updated: 2025-11-05)

Welcome to UPI Vaani – Voice Alerts for Every UPI Transaction (“Application” or “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information while providing you a secure, offline, multilingual, and smart UPI voice alert experience. Use of this App constitutes agreement to this policy.

1. App Overview

UPI Vaani is a lightweight voice alert application that announces all incoming and outgoing UPI transactions in real time. The App operates fully offline to convert UPI notifications into voice alerts even when your device screen is off. No account, login, or registration is required. This ensures privacy, simplicity, and convenience for users such as shopkeepers, delivery agents, and daily UPI users.

2. Information Collection

User-Provided Information

The App does not require any personal information such as name, email, phone number, or banking credentials. No sensitive information is collected from the user.

Automatically Collected Information

For improving app functionality, non-personal technical information may be collected anonymously including device type, operating system version, app usage, and crash logs. This data is fully anonymized and cannot identify a user.

Notification Access & Local Storage

The App uses Notification Access to read UPI transaction notifications from your device. This data is stored only locally using Room Database and is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any server or third party. The App does not access SMS or banking apps directly.

Firebase Firestore Usage

The App uses Firebase Firestore as a database for storing and retrieving certain non-personal configuration data, such as app versioning, feature flags, or remote configuration settings, to manage and update the app experience. No UPI transaction data or personally identifiable information (PII) is stored in Firestore.

Advertising (AdMob & Meta Audience Network)

UPI Vaani displays advertisements through Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network (Facebook Ads), sometimes using AdMob Mediation. These services may use unique identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID) to show personalized or non-personalized ads based on your app usage and general location. Both AdMob and Facebook may collect limited, non-personal information for ad targeting, performance measurement, and fraud prevention. You can opt out of personalized ads anytime from your device’s ad settings.

In-App Purchase (Ad Removal)

The App includes an optional In-App Purchase that allows users to remove all advertisements and enjoy an ad-free experience. All payments are securely processed through Google Play Billing. UPI Vaani does not store, process, or have access to any payment information.

Auto-Start Feature

UPI Vaani can optionally auto-start on device boot. This feature can be enabled or disabled by the user. No data is collected during boot-up.

Location Data

The App does not request, collect, or use location information in any form, though AdMob or Facebook Ads may infer general location (e.g., city level) based on your IP address for ad serving purposes.

Internet Usage

Internet access is required for displaying ads (AdMob and Facebook Ads), anonymous analytics and crash reporting via Firebase, fetching data from Firestore, app updates, and minor background checks. No transaction or personal data is sent to the cloud or shared externally, except for data collected by advertising partners for ad-serving purposes.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

  • Firebase Analytics collects anonymized app usage patterns to improve performance. No personal or UPI transaction data is captured.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymized crash reports including device type, OS version, and technical logs to help resolve bugs efficiently.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted services that may collect limited data as per their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Google AdMob (for advertising)
  • Meta Audience Network (Facebook Ads)
  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing (for in-app purchases)

3. User Consent

By granting Notification Access, users consent to local voice alerts being generated for UPI messages. You can revoke this permission at any time via system settings. By continuing to use UPI Vaani, users also consent to the collection of anonymized analytics, crash data, and the data collection practices by AdMob and Facebook Ads for advertising purposes.

By using the app, you also consent to the ad-serving practices of our advertising partners, including Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network, as outlined in their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Retention

All notification data remains on the user’s device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Only anonymized analytics, crash reports, and non-personal configuration data in Firestore may be retained temporarily on Firebase servers for debugging and improvement purposes. Data collected by AdMob or Facebook Ads is retained as per their policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

UPI Vaani is not intended for children under 13 years of age. No personal information is knowingly collected from children. However, we cannot prevent AdMob or Facebook Ads from collecting non-personal data as part of their ad-serving process.

6. Security

All user data is stored locally on the device. We recommend enabling device-level security such as screen locks or encryption. UPI transaction data is never transmitted over the internet or stored in the cloud. We strive to protect the non-personal configuration data stored in Firestore.

7. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to comply with Google Play requirements or improve transparency. Continued use of the app implies acceptance of any updates.
