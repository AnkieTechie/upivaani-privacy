Privacy Policy for UPI Vaani

Effective Date: 2025-08-20 (Updated: 2025-10-26)

Welcome to UPI Vaani – Voice Alerts for Every UPI Transaction (“Application” or “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information while providing you a secure, offline, multilingual, and smart UPI voice alert experience. Use of this App constitutes agreement to this policy.

1. App Overview

UPI Vaani is a lightweight voice alert application that announces all incoming and outgoing UPI transactions in real time. The App operates fully offline to convert UPI notifications into voice alerts even when your device screen is off. No account, login, or registration is required. This ensures privacy, simplicity, and convenience for users such as shopkeepers, delivery agents, and daily UPI users.

2. Information Collection

User-Provided Information

The App does not require any personal information such as name, email, phone number, or banking credentials. No sensitive information is collected from the user.

Automatically Collected Information

For improving app functionality, non-personal technical information may be collected anonymously including device type, operating system version, app usage, and crash logs. This data is fully anonymized and cannot identify a user.

Notification Access & Local Storage

The App uses Notification Access to read UPI transaction notifications from your device. This data is stored only locally using Room Database and is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any server or third party. The App does not access SMS or banking apps directly.

Firebase Firestore Usage

The App uses **Firebase Firestore** as a database for storing and retrieving certain **non-personal configuration data**, such as app versioning, feature flags, or remote configuration settings, to manage and update the app experience. **No UPI transaction data or personally identifiable information (PII) is stored in Firestore.**

AdMob (Advertising)

UPI Vaani displays advertisements from **Google AdMob**. AdMob uses unique identifiers (like your device's advertising ID) to serve personalized ads based on your interests and app usage. This service may collect and use information about your use of this and other applications to provide more relevant advertisements. This collection and use are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Auto-Start Feature

UPI Vaani can optionally auto-start on device boot. This feature can be enabled or disabled by the user. No data is collected during boot-up.

Location Data

The App does not request, collect, or use location information in any form, **though AdMob may infer general location (e.g., city level) based on your IP address for ad serving purposes.**

Internet Usage

Internet access is required for displaying AdMob ads, anonymous analytics and crash reporting via Firebase, fetching data from Firestore, app updates, and minor background checks. **No transaction or personal data is sent to the cloud or shared externally, except for data collected by AdMob for advertising purposes.**

3. User Consent

By granting Notification Access, users consent to local voice alerts being generated for UPI messages. You can revoke this permission at any time via system settings. By continuing to use UPI Vaani, users also consent to the collection of anonymized analytics, crash data, and the data collection practices by AdMob for advertising purposes.

4. Data Retention

All notification data remains on the user’s device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Only anonymized analytics, crash reports, and non-personal configuration data in Firestore may be retained temporarily on Firebase servers for debugging and improvement purposes. Data collected by AdMob is retained as per Google’s policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

UPI Vaani is not intended for children under 13 years of age. No personal information is knowingly collected from children. However, we cannot prevent AdMob from collecting non-personal data as part of its ad-serving process.

6. Security

All user data is stored locally on the device. We recommend enabling device-level security such as screen locks or encryption. UPI transaction data is never transmitted over the internet or stored in the cloud. We strive to protect the non-personal configuration data stored in Firestore.
